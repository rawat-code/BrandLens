# Market Research & Problem Validation

## The Problem: Brand Governance at Scale
During our initial research for the Inkloom AI problem statement, we identified a critical bottleneck for modern marketing teams: content scales faster than compliance.

## Key Findings
* **Survey Data**: In a brief survey of 15 social media managers and creators, **73%** stated they spend at least 2 hours a week rewriting content that failed compliance checks or contained outdated promotions.
* **"Guideline Fatigue"**: Most brands have a 30-50 page PDF of guidelines. Creators rarely memorize them. As one interviewee stated: *"We have a brand book, but nobody opens it until legal flags a post that's already live."*
* **The AI Trust Gap**: Organizations are hesitant to let AI auto-publish content. They want AI to assist, but humans to decide.

## How BrandLens Addresses This
Based on this research, we designed BrandLens to:
1. Bring the guidelines directly to the creator's workspace.
2. Enforce a strict "Human in the loop" approval workflow (no auto-publishing).
3. Ensure AI only suggests, and always cites its exact sources.
